# Product

<!-- impeccable:product-schema 1 -->

## Platform

web

## Users

Potential employers, recruiters, and clients looking to understand Camila's professional profile, skills, and project work.

## Product Purpose

The portfolio presents Camila's profile, capabilities, and projects so visitors can quickly understand what she does, the problems she can help solve, and how to contact her about employment or collaboration opportunities. Success means communicating her value clearly and making contact straightforward across devices.

## Positioning

Camila combines technical thinking, creativity, and practical problem solving. Her background spans programming, technology solutions, process automation, digital marketing, and content management.

## Operating Context

Visitors evaluate the portfolio to understand Camila's fit for a role, client need, or collaboration. They should be able to scan a concise introduction, skills, and concrete projects, then reach contact information easily on desktop or mobile.

## Capabilities and Constraints

- The site presents a professional profile, skills, project work, and contact options.
- Project descriptions should explain the problem, solution, and technologies used when that information is known.
- Do not invent outcomes, metrics, clients, certifications, features, years of experience, or expertise. Do not present work in progress as complete.
- If project details are missing or uncertain, ask Camila rather than filling gaps with assumptions.
- Keep information concise, clear, and strategic; avoid redundant copy and exhaustive technology lists.
- Preserve the existing React, TypeScript, Vite, and Tailwind CSS 4 implementation.

## Brand Commitments

- Present Camila as a current, versatile, creative, technically minded professional with initiative and visual judgment.
- Keep the information brief, clear, and strategic.
- The user requests a modern, minimal, carefully considered identity with fluid transitions and restrained, useful microinteractions.

## Evidence on Hand

- A representative project described by Camila is a WhatsApp support chatbot integrated with GLPI, using n8n, APIs, PostgreSQL, and Gemini. The portfolio must not imply undocumented results or features.
- The repository contains a FoodStore project detail surface and related screenshots/assets. Treat these as existing portfolio material; do not infer undocumented outcomes or project status.
- Preserve real projects, technologies, and experience from Camila's trajectory. Do not fabricate testimonials, customer names, metrics, or credentials.

## Product Principles

- Help visitors understand Camila's professional fit quickly.
- Demonstrate capabilities through real project examples and clear explanations.
- Represent technical and creative work as a coherent, versatile profile.
- Make navigation and contact clear and usable across devices.
- Keep claims accurate and grounded in documented experience.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

The portfolio should be responsive, accessible, fast, and easy to navigate. Contact should remain visible and simple to use on mobile and desktop.
